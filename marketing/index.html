Why we exist

Nigeria grows the palm. It doesn’t yet process it well.

Palm kernels are one of the most valuable parts of the oil palm, and southeastern Nigeria produces them in large quantities. Most are still processed in small, informal mills, where output quality varies from batch to batch, records are rare and much of the value is lost before it reaches a buyer.

For manufacturers, that means inconsistent supply and uncertain quality. For farmers, it means less of the value they grow. Oriafo is built to close that gap: a formal, well-run processing operation, close to where the kernels are grown, that buyers can rely on.

Responsible processing

Less waste, cleaner power.

Every part of the kernel has a use: oil for manufacturing, cake for feed and shells for fuel. We aim to sell or reuse all of it rather than discard it. Our site runs on solar power, and we’re exploring turning palm kernel shells into biochar, a material that stores carbon in soil and improves its fertility.
